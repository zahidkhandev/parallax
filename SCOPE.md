# Scope

## Included event

This repository analyses media coverage directly related to the **NEET 2026 protests**.

A source is eligible when its principal subject, or a clearly identifiable segment within it, concerns the protest, its stated demands, official responses, policing, political reactions, legal developments, public impact, or disputed claims about the event.

## Included media

- Television news reports
- Television debates and panel discussions
- Digital-first news videos
- Verified news-channel uploads
- Official interviews published by news organisations
- Relevant headline, thumbnail and description packaging
- Clearly identified portions of mixed or long-form streams

## Included actors

The dataset may include coverage concerning:

- Protest organisers
- Protest participants
- Students and candidates
- Parents
- Government and ministry representatives
- Exam and education authorities
- Police and security agencies
- Political parties and representatives
- Courts, commissions and public institutions
- Journalists, anchors, correspondents and guests
- Independent experts
- Other directly relevant entities

## Excluded

- Unrelated political coverage
- General NEET preparation or counselling content
- Videos that merely contain a matching keyword without covering the protest
- Anonymous social-media claims without a verifiable source
- Private personal data
- Face recognition or identity inference for ordinary participants
- Unverified copies where the original source cannot be established
- Complete downloaded broadcasts in the public repository

## Time window

The precise collection start and end dates must be recorded in `public-data/collection-manifest.json`.

Changing the time window requires a documented methodology version change.

## Neutrality rule

Inclusion does not depend on whether coverage is favourable or critical toward any actor. The same evidence and review standards apply across outlets, speakers and targets.
