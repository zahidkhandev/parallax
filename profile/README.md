# Project Parallax

**Open-source, evidence-first analysis of media framing around the NEET 2026 protests.**

Project Parallax examines how different news outlets frame protesters, students, government bodies, exam authorities, police, political actors and institutions.

We do not begin with a predetermined political conclusion. Public findings are tied to timestamps, speaker attribution, short evidence excerpts, published definitions and review status.

## Current repository

**NEET Protest 2026 Media Analysis**

The current work is intentionally limited to one event. It measures:

- Who receives favourable or critical framing
- Which targets receive the strongest negative attention
- Which voices are included or excluded
- How allegations and disputed claims are qualified
- Whether editorial packaging is supported by programme content
- How evidence quality affects conclusions

## Independence

Project Parallax is independent and is not affiliated with any protest organisation, government body, examination authority, political party, police organisation or media outlet.
